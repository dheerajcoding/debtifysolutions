Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will checking my options hurt my credit score?

No. Debtify uses a soft credit inquiry, which does not affect your score. During a settlement program your score usually drops because you stop paying creditors while you save.

When do I pay fees?

Only after a debt is settled and you've made at least one payment on that settlement. The fee is a percentage of the enrolled debt and is shown before you sign.

How long does a program take?

Most programs take 24 to 48 months. Your estimate is shown before you sign.

Can creditors still call or sue me?

Yes. Creditors may keep collecting and may take legal action while debts are being negotiated. If you're sued, tell your program company right away.

Is Debtify a debt settlement company?

No. Debtify is a technology platform. Your program, agreement and negotiations come from a licensed partner company, named before you sign.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. The money in your dedicated account is yours, less any account fees.

Ready to see your options?

About 5 minutes. No sales call. You decide.
